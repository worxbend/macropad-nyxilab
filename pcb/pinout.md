## Parts per board

### left

- J1: FPC 20-pin 0.5 mm, to the hat
- J2: JST-SH 8, bar display pigtail
- J3: JST-SH 5, encoder (rotary edition; not fitted on the joystick edition)
- C1: 100 nF 0402, 3V3 at J1
- 4 MX switches (soldered, or Mill-Max 0305 sockets) and as many 1N4148W diodes (SOD-123): column -> switch -> diode anode, cathode -> row (COL2ROW)

### right

- J1: FPC 20-pin 0.5 mm, to the hat
- J2: JST-SH 8, main display pigtail
- C1: 100 nF 0402, 3V3 at J1
- 8 MX switches (soldered, or Mill-Max 0305 sockets) and as many 1N4148W diodes (SOD-123): column -> switch -> diode anode, cathode -> row (COL2ROW)

### hat

- J1: FPC 20-pin 0.5 mm, from the left board
- J2: FPC 20-pin 0.5 mm, from the right board
- J3: JST-SH 5, joystick (joystick edition; not fitted on the rotary edition)
- J4: JST-PH 3, LED sticks
- D1: Schottky 1 A SOD-123 (B5819W class), VBUS -> LED_5V
- U1: 74AHCT1G125 SOT-23-5: 3.3 V data -> 5 V data
- R1: 330 R 0402, LED data series resistor
- C1: 10 uF 0805, LED_5V bulk
- C2: 100 nF 0402, U1 supply
- C3: 100 nF 0402, 3V3 at J1
- C4: 100 nF 0402, 3V3 at J2
- H1: 2 x 20 pin header 2.54 mm to the Pico 2's pin rows (the Pico's own pinout)
